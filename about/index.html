About

Partnering with our neighbors in Mishawaka

Next Steps Community Center is being built at 1550 McKinley Ave in Mishawaka, at the corner of McKinley and Fir. It's a ministry of Granger Community Church, three and a half miles south of the church's Granger campus.

Taking one next step, one relationship at a time

Our hope is to partner with our neighbors in Mishawaka and the surrounding area, meet real needs and take steps toward Christ together. We picture a place that supports all five spheres of a person's life:

  • Spiritual
  • Physical
  • Mental
  • Financial
  • Social

Early ideas

Nothing is set yet. These are some of the ideas we started with. What the center actually offers, and when, depends on what we hear from neighbors.

  • Food pantry

    A welcoming front door for meeting everyday needs.

  • Garden

    A shared, hands-on space where neighbors could grow food together.

  • Teaching kitchen

    A place to learn practical skills like cooking healthy meals and stretching a budget.

  • Outdoor activity space

    Safe space for kids and families, especially in the after-school hours.

The site covers 5.5 acres, with four schools within a few miles, so there's room to grow as we learn what our community needs most.

We're starting by listening

Before the doors open, we want to hear from you. Through short surveys, neighborhood gatherings and focus groups, neighbors will help shape what Next Steps Community Center becomes.
