About

Partnering with our neighbors in Mishawaka

Next Steps Community Center is being built at 1550 McKinley Ave in Mishawaka, at the corner of McKinley and Fir. It's a ministry of Granger Community Church, three and a half miles south of the church's Granger campus.

Taking one next step, one relationship at a time

Our hope is to partner with our neighbors in Mishawaka and the surrounding area, meet real needs and take steps toward Christ together. The Next Steps Program will support all five spheres of a person's life:

  • Spiritual
  • Physical
  • Mental
  • Financial
  • Social

What's planned

  • Food pantry

    A welcoming front door for meeting tangible needs.

  • Garden

    Food grown on site, in a shared, hands-on space where neighbors work alongside each other.

  • Teaching kitchen

    Practical skills you can carry home: preparing healthy food, stretching a budget and supporting a full and healthy life.

  • Outdoor activity space

    Safe, high-quality space for the after-school hours, when many working families in Mishawaka need support most.

The site covers 5.5 acres, with four schools within a few miles. As programming grows, we expect even more ways to help meet our community's needs.

We're starting by listening

Before the doors open, we want to hear from you. Through short surveys, neighborhood gatherings and focus groups, neighbors will help shape what Next Steps Community Center becomes.
